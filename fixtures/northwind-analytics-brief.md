# Northwind Analytics — Investment Brief

## Business

Northwind Analytics builds demand forecasting software for grocery retailers. The company was spun out of a university research group in 2016 and now serves 42 retail chains.

## Registered Office

Northwind's registered office is in the King Abdullah Financial District, Tower 14, Riyadh. The company redomiciled there in 2020 after two years of operating from a shared workspace in Jeddah. The move was driven by proximity to its three largest customers.

A satellite engineering office remains in Jeddah with 28 staff. A commercial office opened in Dubai Internet City in 2023, staffed by nine people covering the northern accounts.

## Ownership

The founders retain 46 percent. A growth equity fund holds 39 percent following a Series B in 2022. The remaining 15 percent sits with employees under a phantom share scheme.

## Financials

Annual recurring revenue reached 96 million Saudi Riyal in 2024, growing 31 percent. Gross margin is 78 percent. The company reached operating breakeven in the third quarter of 2024.

Net cash at year end stood at 44 million Saudi Riyal. The board does not expect to raise again before 2027.

## Leadership

Chief executive Mariam Suleiman co-founded the company and previously led forecasting at a regional grocery group. The chief technology officer is Yusuf Barakat. Finance is overseen on a part-time basis by an external controller.

## Risks

Customer concentration remains the principal risk: the three largest accounts represent 51 percent of recurring revenue. Contract terms are annual, with 60-day termination notice.

A second risk is talent retention in the engineering office, where attrition ran at 19 percent in 2024.
