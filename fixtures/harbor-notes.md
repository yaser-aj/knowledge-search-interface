# Harbor Workshop Notes

The north dock stores cedar planks for the small-boat repair crew.
Cedar is reserved for hull patches; oak is reserved for keels.

Quiet Harbor's winter schedule closes the public slip on Tuesdays
so the crane can move masts. Visitors should use the east walkway
on those days.

The spare bronze fasteners live in bin 14B, next to the tar pots.
Do not mix them with the steel screws in bin 9.

Tea is brewed at 10:30 in the loft. The kettle is not to be used
for melting pitch.
