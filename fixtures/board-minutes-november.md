# Board Minutes — 14 November

## Present

The Chair, the chief executive, the finance chief, the chief operating officer, and both independent directors. The general counsel attended as secretary.

## 1. Minutes of the Previous Meeting

Approved without amendment.

## 2. Office Relocation

The chief operating officer presented the case for relocating the northern commercial team. The current serviced space in Dubai Internet City is at capacity, and the lease expires in June.

Two options were tabled. The first is a larger floor in Business Bay, at a 22 percent higher annual cost. The second is a move to Sharjah, which reduces cost by 9 percent but adds roughly 40 minutes of daily travel for most of the team.

The Chair noted that client meetings are concentrated in the Dubai financial district and cautioned against the Sharjah option on that basis. The board approved the Business Bay lease, subject to the finance chief confirming the capital cost of the fit-out.

## 3. Treasury Update

Abdo reported that cash conversion improved by six days in the quarter. The Manama account was consolidated into the main treasury structure, closing a legacy facility that had carried an unused commitment fee.

He flagged that the Kuwait City supplier relationship remains on prepayment terms pending a credit review.

## 4. Audit Committee Report

Helena Vogt reported that the interim audit raised no material findings. Two low-severity observations concerned access controls in the warehouse management system, both with agreed remediation dates.

## 5. Risk Register

The register was reviewed. Customer concentration was retained as the highest scored risk. Fleet insurance renewal was added as an emerging item.

## 6. Any Other Business

The chief executive noted an approach from an adviser regarding a possible acquisition of a Jeddah-based last-mile operator. The board asked for a short options paper at the next meeting.

## Close

The meeting closed at 15:40. The next meeting is scheduled for 12 February.
