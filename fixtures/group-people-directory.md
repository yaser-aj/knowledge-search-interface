## Signing Authority

Payments above 2 million require two signatures, one of which must be the group chief financial officer or, in his absence, the chief executive.

Bank mandates are held jointly by the finance chief and the general counsel. The finance chief may delegate approval up to 500,000 to the group treasurer for a maximum of ten working days.

## Delegated Limits

| Role | Single transaction limit |
| --- | --- |
| Group treasurer | 500,000 |
| Chief operating officer | 1,000,000 |

| Chief executive officer | 10,000,000 |

## Contact Protocol

Media enquiries go to the general counsel. Investor enquiries go to the finance chief. Regulatory correspondence is logged by the company secretary within one working day of receipt.
