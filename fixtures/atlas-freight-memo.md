# Atlas Freight Holdings — Internal Memo

## Purpose

This memo summarises the proposed restructuring of Atlas Freight Holdings ahead of the March board meeting.

## Corporate Seat

Atlas Freight Holdings B.V. has its statutory seat in Rotterdam, in the Netherlands. The group's principal place of business is the Waalhaven office complex, where 310 staff are based. There is no intention to move the corporate seat as part of this restructuring.

Secondary offices operate in Antwerp, Hamburg, and Felixstowe. A representative office in Singapore covers Asian shipping lines.

## Proposed Structure

The proposal collapses three intermediate holding entities into a single Dutch parent. Operating subsidiaries in Belgium and Germany would be held directly. The expected annual saving in administration and audit cost is 1.9 million euro.

## Trade Lanes

Atlas is strongest on North Sea and Baltic routes. The group has no Middle East operations and no Gulf presence, having exited its Jebel Ali agency arrangement in 2021 after four unprofitable years.

## Financials

Revenue for 2024 was 780 million euro, flat year on year. Operating margin compressed to 4.1 percent from 5.6 percent, driven by rate softness on Baltic routes.

## Leadership

The chief executive is Pieter van Dijk. Finance is led by Sofie Maes. The supervisory board is chaired by Henk Willems.

## Recommendation

Management recommends the board approve the restructuring in principle and authorise the legal work, with completion targeted for the fourth quarter.
