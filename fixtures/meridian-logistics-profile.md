# Meridian Logistics Group — Company Profile

## Overview

Meridian Logistics Group is a freight forwarding and warehousing firm founded in 2011. The group operates a fleet of 240 vehicles and four bonded warehouses, and employs 1,180 staff across its network.

## Head Office

The group's head office occupies the eighth and ninth floors of the Al Sadd Tower on Al Sadd Street in Doha. All treasury, legal, and executive functions are run from this office. The registered entity is Meridian Logistics W.L.L., incorporated locally in 2011 and licensed by the Ministry of Commerce and Industry.

Rent for the head office is settled annually in Qatari Riyal. The lease runs to March 2031 with a five-year extension option.

## Regional Presence

Beyond the head office, Meridian maintains operating branches in:

- Al Wakrah, serving the southern industrial corridor
- Mesaieed, adjacent to the port
- Manama, opened in 2019 to serve the northern Gulf trade lane
- Muscat, opened in 2022, focused on overland freight to the interior

The Manama branch reports into the head office directly. The Muscat branch reports through the regional operations director.

## Financial Summary

Revenue for the 2024 financial year reached 512 million Qatari Riyal, up 14 percent year on year. Warehousing contributed 38 percent of revenue, road freight 44 percent, and customs brokerage the remainder.

Operating margin held at 11.2 percent. The group carries modest leverage, with a net debt to EBITDA ratio of 1.4 times.

## Leadership

The group is led by chief executive Nadia Haddad, appointed in 2018. Abdulrahman Al-Otaibi serves as group chief financial officer. The board is chaired by Faisal Bin Rashid Al-Thani, a founding shareholder.

## Certifications

Meridian holds ISO 9001 and ISO 45001 certification. The bonded warehouse network is accredited for pharmaceutical cold chain handling, audited annually by an external assessor.
