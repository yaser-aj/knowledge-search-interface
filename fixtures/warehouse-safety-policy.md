# Warehouse Safety Policy

## Scope

This policy applies to all bonded and ambient warehouse facilities operated by the group, and to contractors working on those sites.

## Personal Protective Equipment

High-visibility vests and safety footwear are mandatory in all racking aisles and loading bays. Cut-resistant gloves are required when handling banded pallets. Hearing protection is required in the shrink-wrap enclosure.

## Forklift Operation

Only staff holding a current counterbalance certificate may operate a forklift. Certificates are revalidated every three years. Pedestrian routes are marked in yellow and must not be obstructed by staged pallets.

Speed within the building is limited to 8 kilometres per hour. Horns must be sounded at every blind corner.

## Cold Chain Handling

Pharmaceutical cold chain stock is held between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius. Temperature is logged continuously, with alarms routed to the duty supervisor's handset. Any excursion beyond 15 minutes triggers a quarantine of the affected pallet and a deviation report within 24 hours.

## Incident Reporting

All incidents, including near misses, must be reported to the site supervisor before the end of the shift. Reportable injuries are escalated to the chief operating officer within two hours.

The group target is zero lost-time injuries. The 2024 outturn was two lost-time injuries, both sprains associated with manual handling.

## Training

Every warehouse employee completes induction training before first unsupervised shift, and refresher training annually. Training records are held in the warehouse management system and audited twice a year.

## Review

This policy is reviewed annually by the chief operating officer and approved by the executive committee.
